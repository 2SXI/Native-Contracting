About the Company

Built on-site,
not on paper.

From foundation to final coat, Native Construction runs every project with our own supervised crews — the same team that draws the plan is accountable for the brickwork, the paving, and the finish you walk into.

Native Construction (trading as Native Contracting) is a full-service building and civil works contractor based at 55 Lawley Avenue, Belvedere, Harare. We take on projects most contractors split across three companies: architectural design and bills of quantities, structural building, civil and electrical works, and the finishing trades that determine how a space actually feels to live and work in.

Our crews work in branded, high-visibility PPE on every site, and every job — from a driveway to a full renovation — is registered and run to NSSA, ZIMRA, NEC and Construction Industry Federation of Zimbabwe standards. That's not paperwork for its own sake; it's how we protect our people and your investment.

How a Project Runs

From plan to handover.

A fixed sequence keeps every build predictable — for us and for you.

Stage 1 — Consult

Site Visit & Brief

We assess the site, understand your brief and budget, and scope what's possible.

Stage 2 — Design

Drawings & BOQ

Architectural drawings and a bill of quantities you can hold us to, line by line.

Stage 3 — Build

Structural Works

Foundation, brickwork, roofing and civil works, run by our own supervised crew.

Stage 4 — Finish

Tiling, Paint & Fit-out

Aluminium, tiling, painting and landscaping — the details that get lived in.

Stage 5 — Handover

Walkthrough & Sign-off

A final walkthrough against the BOQ, snag list closed, keys handed over.
