About the Company

Built to
perfection.

From foundation excavation to final coat, Native Construction runs every project with our own supervised crews, working to engineering drawings and specification — in Harare, Zimbabwe.

Native Contracting Zimbabwe (trading as Native Construction) is a full-service building and civil works contractor based at Suite B, Number 1 Waterfield Road, Mt Pleasant, Harare. We take on projects most contractors split across three companies: architectural design and bills of quantities, structural building and civil engineering, and the finishing trades — carried through from setting-out and foundation excavation to roof structure, screed and plaster, and final fit-out.

Our crews work in branded, high-visibility PPE on every site, under direct site supervision and to engineering drawings and specification, with every job registered and run to NSSA, ZIMRA, NEC and Construction Industry Federation of Zimbabwe standards.

How a Project Runs

From plan to handover.

A fixed sequence keeps every build predictable and defensible — for us and for you.

Stage 1 — Consult

Site Visit & Brief

We assess the site — access, soil conditions and services — understand your brief and budget, and scope what's structurally possible.

Stage 2 — Design

Drawings & BOQ

Architectural drawings and a detailed bill of quantities to guide your budget and scope, line by line.

Stage 3 — Build

Structural Works

Setting-out, foundation excavation, brickwork, roof structure and civil works, run by our own supervised crew to specification.

Stage 4 — Finish

Tiling, Paint & Fit-out

Aluminium works, screeding, tiling, painting and landscaping — the details that get lived in.

Stage 5 — Handover

Walkthrough & Sign-off

A final walkthrough against the BOQ and drawings, snag list closed, keys handed over.

Registration & Compliance

Every project runs under formal registration — so you're covered, not exposed.
