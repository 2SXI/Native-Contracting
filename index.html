About the Company

Built on-site,
not on paper.

From foundation to final coat, Native Construction runs every project with our own supervised crews in Harare, Zimbabwe.

Native Contracting Zimbabwe (trading as Native Construction) has been building in Harare since 2018, from a site office at 55 Lawley Avenue, Belvedere. Where most projects need three separate companies — a designer, a builder, and a finishing crew — we run all of it under one roof: architectural design and bills of quantities, structural and civil works, and the finishing trades.

Every crew works in branded, high-visibility PPE, and every job is registered and run to NSSA, ZIMRA, NEC and Construction Industry Federation of Zimbabwe standards.

How a Project Runs

From plan to handover.

A fixed sequence keeps every build predictable — for us and for you.

Stage 1 — Consult

Site Visit & Brief

We assess the site, understand your brief and budget, and scope what's possible.

Stage 2 — Design

Drawings & BOQ

Architectural drawings and a detailed bill of quantities to guide your budget and scope, line by line.

Stage 3 — Build

Structural Works

Foundation, brickwork, roofing and civil works, run by our own supervised crew.

Stage 4 — Finish

Tiling, Paint & Fit-out

Aluminium, tiling, painting and landscaping — the details that get lived in.

Stage 5 — Handover

Walkthrough & Sign-off

A final walkthrough against the BOQ, snag list closed, keys handed over.
