About the Company

Built on-site, not on paper.

Native Construction is a Harare-based building and civil works contractor delivering architectural design, structural building, and finishing trades under one accountable crew.

Native Contracting Zimbabwe (trading as Native Construction) is a full-service building and civil works contractor based at Suite B, Number 1 Waterfield Road, Mt Pleasant, Harare. We take on projects most contractors split across three companies: architectural design and bills of quantities, structural building, civil and electrical works, and the finishing trades that determine how a space actually feels to live and work in.

Our crews work in branded, high-visibility PPE on every site, and every job — from a driveway to a full renovation — is registered and run to NSSA, ZIMRA, NEC and Construction Industry Federation of Zimbabwe standards. That's not paperwork for its own sake; it's how we protect our people and your investment.
